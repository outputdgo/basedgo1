ABOUT US

Founded in 2024, Output DGO (Digital Graphic Operations) is a Midwest-based digital design studio specializing in custom web experiences. Founded by Marcus Hazley and Devyn Johnson, the studio develops websites and digital solutions that give businesses and creators full control over how they connect with their audiences. Instead of relying on algorithms or third-party platforms, Output DGO builds independent digital ecosystems—spaces where brands own their presence and engage users directly. The studio’s work focuses on clarity, performance, and long-term impact, helping clients establish lasting connections.
